Questions

What is Mandala Finance?⌄

A DeFi platform on Ethereum. Swap across aggregated liquidity, stake tokens or LP positions for rewards, and launch a token with a market attached to it. Swapping and staking are live today. Token launches are open to approved issuers only, and lending and borrowing are still in development.

How does routing work?⌄

Every swap is priced across several venues at once, ranked, and returned to you as a route. Execution runs through Mandala’s own router, and you see the sources, the fees and the price impact before you sign anything.

Is my capital at risk?⌄

Yes. Mandala is software, not a bank. You connect your own wallet and sign every transaction yourself, and nothing here is insured or guaranteed. Smart contracts can contain defects, prices move against you, and a token launched through the platform can lose all of its value. Our contracts have been through external security review and independent retesting — [LINK: published reports]. Never commit more than you can afford to lose.

What chains are supported?⌄

Ethereum mainnet. Mandala is built so further networks can be added without rearchitecting, and we announce new chains when they are live rather than before.
